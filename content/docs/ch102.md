**縮寫對照：**

- **AMB**: Amphotericin B
- **FLU**: Fluconazole
- **ITR**: Itraconazole
- **POS**: Posaconazole
- **VOR**: Voriconazole
- **ANI**: Anidulafungin
- **MFG**: Micafungin
- **CAS**: Caspofungin
- **5FC**: Flucytosine
