    ICU常用藥物配法 (ICU Common Drug Formulations)#

    常用單位換算： 60 MD (microdrip) = 60 gtt = 1 ml, 1 gtt/min = 1 ml/hr

    註： 此處in N/S 250ml代表泡成總量為250ml之N/S溶液

    升壓劑 (若用濃的泡法需由CVC給予)#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Dobutamine 250mg/20cc/Amp. 劑量：2-20 μg/kg/min, Initial 2.5-5 μg/kg/min 稀2 amp in D5W 250ml = 2000 μg/ml Max rate = BW × 0.6
    Dopamine 200mg/5cc/Amp. Premix = 400mg/250ml 稀2 amp in D5W 250ml = 1600 μg/ml; 濃4amp in D5W 250ml = 3200 μg/ml 劑量：1-5 μg/kg/min dopaminergic; 5-10 μg/kg/min β-adrenergic; 10-20 μg/kg/min α-adrenergic. Max rate = 20 × BW / 53.33 (濃) 約20ml/min
    Epinephrine 1mg/cc/Amp 濃6 amp in N/S 100ml = 60 μg/ml = 1 μg/ml/min 劑量：2-20 μg/min. Max rate = 20
    Levophed (Norepinephrine) 4mg/4cc/Amp 濃4 amp in D5W 250ml = 64 μg/ml = 1.06 μg/ml/min 劑量：1-40 μg/min。Max rate = 38，但一般很少超過20-25。
    Vasopressin (Pitressin) 20U/1ml/amp 濃3amp in N/S 100ml = 0.6U/ml = 0.01U/ml/min 劑量：0.01-0.03U/min. Max rate = 3ml/hr

    心律不整#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Amiodarone 150mg/3cc/Amp. 劑量：150-300mg loading 10min then 1mg/min × 6hr then 0.5mg/min × 18hr 6 amp in D5W 450ml = 2mg/ml 30 gtt/min × 6hr then 15 gtt/min × 18hr
    Amiodarone 6 amp in D5W 500ml = 1.8mg/ml 34 gtt/min × 6hr then 17 gtt/min × 18hr
    Lidocaine 2% 20mg/ml, 5ml/Vial 10amp in NS or D5W 250ml = 4mg/ml 劑量：1mg/kg loading, Maintain 1-4mg/min. Max Rate = 60
    Isoproterenol 0.2mg/cc/Amp 5 amp in D5W or NS 250ml = 4 μg/ml 劑量：2-10 μg/min. Rate = 欲run dose × 15

    急性心肌梗塞，心絞痛，心臟衰竭#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Isosorbide dinitrate (Isoket) 10mg/amp 50mg in D5W 500ml = 0.1 mg/ml (瓶裝 D5W) 劑量：1-10mg/hr. Rate = dose × 10
    NTG (Millisrol) 5mg/10ml/amp, 50mg/100ml/bottle 10 amp 純汁 = 1btl = 0.5 mg/ml = 8.33 μg/ml/min 劑量：10-20 μg/min起始，每5-10 min增加5-10 μg/min直到症狀緩解，或BP降到目標。3 gtt起始 ↑↓ 1-2 gtt Q5 min
    Nitroprusside 50mg/vial 2 vial in D5W 250ml = 200 μg/ml (瓶裝 D5W，避光) 劑量：0.5-10 μg/kg/min. Rate = 欲run dose × BW/3.3
    Heparin 25000/5cc/Vial 25000 U in D5W or NS 250ml = 100 U/ml 劑量：60 U/kg loading, Max 4000U. 12 U/kg/hr drip 48hr. Q6H測aPTT

    高血壓#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Labetalol (Trandate) 25mg/5ml/amp 8 amp in NS or D5W 100ml = 2 mg/ml 20 mg over 2 min then 20-80 mg q10min or 10-120 mg/h. Max rate = 60

    ICU鎮靜劑#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Midazolam (Dormicum) 5mg/5ml/Amp 12 amp in NS 60ml = 1 mg/ml 劑量：Loading 0.03-0.3mg/kg (0.1mg/kg), Maintain 0.05-0.1mg/kg/hr. 5 mg loading, Max Rate = BW × 0.1
    Propofol 200mg/20ml/Amp 不須稀釋，已經是10mg/ml。 劑量：Loading 0.25-1mg/kg, Maintain 1-6mg/kg/hr. BW/10 gtt起始，視sedation情況調整。Rate = dose × BW/10
    Fentanyl 0.5 mg/10 ml/amp 1 amp in N/S 50ml = 10 μg/ml 劑量：50-100 μg/h，但上限可更高。1ml/hr起始
    Cisatracurium (Nimbex) 10mg/5ml/amp 10 amp in N/S 250ml = 0.4mg/ml 劑量：0.5-10 μg/kg/min

    DKA/HHS#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Insulin (RI) 50U in NS 500ml = 0.1U/ml 幾公斤run幾gtt
    Insulin (RI) 100U NS 100ml = 1U/ml BW/10 gtt
    劑量：Loading 0.2U/kg, Maintain 0.1U/kg/hr，依BS調整。

    消化道出血#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Terlipressin (Glypressin) 1mg/vial 不須continuous run Loading：2mg Q4-6H到24小時無出血，Maintain：1mg Q4-6H x 3-5 days. 2 Amp ST然後2 amp Q4-6H直到連續24小時沒有出血，然後1 amp Q4-6H x 5天。
    Somatostatin (Somatosan) 3mg/amp 2 amp in N/S 500ml = 12 μg/ml Loading：250 μg, Maintain：250 μg/h. 20ml bolus 3min then run 20 gtt
    Octreotide (Sandostatin) 0.1mg/1ml/amp 10 amp in N/S 200ml = 5 μg/ml Loading：50-100 μg, Maintain：25-50 μg/h. 1 amp ST then run 10 gtt
    Nexium 40mg/amp 2 amp in N/S 100ml = 5 amp in N/S 250ml = 80mg/100ml Loading: 80mg/30min, Maintain: 8mg/hr x 3 days. Run 10 gtt

    利尿劑#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Furosemide (Lasix) 20mg/2cc/Amp 純汁 = 10 mg/ml 劑量：Loading 30 × cre mg, Maintain 20-160 mg/h. Max rate = 16，不過很少用到這麼高。

    抗血小板/抗凝血#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Tirofiban (Aggrastat) 12.5mg/50ml/amp 1 amp in NS 200ml = 50 μg/ml 0.4 μg/kg/min for 30 minutes → 0.1 μg/kg/min. BW × 0.48 for 30min → BW × 0.12. Ccr < 50 or Cre > 2，dose減半。

    心臟用藥#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Milrinone (Primacor) 10mg/10ml/vial 20mg in D5W / NS 100ml = 200 μg/ml = 3.33 μg/ml/min 50 μg/kg over 10 min then 0.375-0.75 μg/kg/min. Max Rate = 0.225 × BW

    血栓溶解#

    藥物 劑量
    r-tPA 15 (loading) → 50 (30min) → 35 (60min)

    支氣管痙攣#

    藥物 劑量 配法 滴速
    Aminophylline 250mg/10ml/amp 7 mg/kg bolus, 0.4 mg/kg/hr maintain 2 amp in N/S 250ml = 2 mg/ml. 2 amp ST then run 0.2 × BW gtt
