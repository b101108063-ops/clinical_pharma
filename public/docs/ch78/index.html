    CH78 促腸胃蠕動藥物 (Prokinetic Drugs)#

    5-HT₄ 致效劑 PO#

    Drug Brand Name Indications Side Effects
    Cisapride 胃輕癱 (Gastroparesis)、GERD S/E: 藥物交互作用、QT prolong、torsades de pointes
    Mosapride Mopride 取代 cisapride S/E: Nonspecific abd pain, dry mouth、↑TG (少)、↑AST/ALT (少)

    5-HT₄ 致效劑 + D₂-R 拮抗劑 IV, PO#

    Drug Brand Name Indications Side Effects
    Metoclopramide Primperan 胃輕癱、functional dyspepsia (促進小腸>大腸)、止吐。用於避免胃脹 S/E: EPS (尤其是老人)。在 Parkinsonism 的病人要小心使用

    周邊 D₂-R 阻斷#

    Drug Indications
    Domperidone 胃輕癱、functional dyspepsia (促進大腸>小腸)、Parkinsonism 患者服用 L-dopa 引起的噁心嘔吐

    Motilin Agonist#

    Drug Indications Dosage
    Erythromycin UGI bleeding 做 panendoscope 之前使用可使視野更清楚 250mg IVD for 30 min。250 mg Q8H IV，用 1-2 天，無效就停藥。

    Somatostatin Analogue#

    Drug Indications
    Octreotide Intestinal pseudoobstruction

    膽鹼性致效劑#

    Drug Mechanism Dosage & S/E
    Bethanechol ↑ Myenteric plexus → ↑ Ach 全身均有作用、S/E 大
    Neostigmine 0.25 mg Q4-6H SC，用 1-2 天，無效就停藥
